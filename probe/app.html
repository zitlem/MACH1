Put it into a part program

Open the Mazatrol part program (.MAZ, .PBM or .PBD) this probing is for: the units this job needs go straight into it, after the units you pick. Keep a copy of the original.

Inspection report

Inspected features print their results to print.txt on the control (C:\ymw\M8Y\data\MC_sdg\print, with data I/O parameter DPR14 = 4). Copy it here and open it, with the job that made the program open.

The cycles' inputs follow the macro packs they were written for (Renishaw A-4013-0113-0T; Mazak MMS V1.0). Always run a new probing program single block with the rapid and feed overrides low, and watch the first touches.

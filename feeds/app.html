Feeds and Speeds

Speeds and feeds for high-efficiency machining: end mills (HEM, profiling, slotting, finish and spring passes), ball and tapered end mills, any coating, helical and circular interpolation of bores, high-feed, face and chamfer mills, drills, taps and reamers. Chip thinning, power, torque and tool deflection are worked out and checked against your machine. Inch.

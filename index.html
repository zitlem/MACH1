Tools for Mazatrol programs that run in your browser. Nothing to install, and nothing is uploaded: files you open stay on your computer.

Mazatrol Editor

Open, edit, check and plot Mazatrol and EIA/ISO programs, with the control's soft keys, cycle times, priority run order, TPC, program check and a G/M-code and alarm reference.

Speeds and feeds for HEM and every tool type, with chip thinning, power, deflection and tap drills.
